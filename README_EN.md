<details>
  <summary><strong>📖 About Me</strong></summary>
  <br>
  I work at the intersection of <strong>applied economics, data science, and public policy evaluation</strong>.  

  My research focuses on how information, regulation, and spatial structures shape firm behavior and regional development through geoeconomic analysis. Currently, I coordinate data infrastructure and analytics projects that connect Brazilian administrative microdata (e.g., RAIS, CAGED, CNPJ) with spatial analysis for economic monitoring in Espírito Santo, Brazil.

  In short: <em>I study how data reveals socioeconomic dynamics that public policies often overlook.</em>
</details>

<details>
  <summary><strong>🧭 Ongoing Projects</strong></summary>
  <br>
  <h3>Espírito Santo Business Observatory</h3>
  An open data initiative combining firm-level records (CNPJ), administrative microdata (RAIS/CAGED), and spatial indicators to monitor economic activity across municipalities and sectors.  
  Tools: Python, SQL, Looker Studio, BigQuery.
  <br>
  <a href="https://lookerstudio.google.com/reporting/c343406e-dbb1-41b7-a2db-56b4801d5101/page/p_eo722dulld">Dashboard Link</a>

  <h3>ZEEC-ES — Coastal Ecological-Economic Zoning</h3>
  Designing a geospatial framework for the planning of coastal regions in Espírito Santo, integrating environmental, connectivity, and labor indicators.  
  Focus: georeferenced modeling, infrastructure mapping, and economic clusters.

  <h3>BCB Focus Report Expectations Tracker</h3>
  A dashboard that tracks weekly market forecasts from the Focus Report against real-time economic data (GDP, Interest Rate, Inflation, and Exchange Rate).
  <br>
  <a href="https://github.com/galvd/bcb_focus_expectations/blob/main/charts/index.md">Dashboard Link with Charts</a>

  <h3>Uncertain Politics, Predictable Consumption?</h3>
  Working paper under review at <em>Emerging Markets Review</em>.  
  Explores how political uncertainty affects consumption patterns and labor market responses in Latin America.
  <br>
  <a href="https://dx.doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.5510940">SSRN Link</a>
</details>
